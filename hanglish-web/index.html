How to use Hanglish

Typing

  • Each block has three rows. The three block buttons choose the row: top = consonants before the vowel, middle = vowel (and grammar marks), bottom = consonants after it.
  • Tap space once to move to the next row. Tap it twice quickly to end the word.
  • The two-boxes key starts the next block in the same word.
  • Delete removes the last thing you typed; hold it to keep deleting.
  • 123 has digits and punctuation.

Sending

  • Tap the picture key, choose WhatsApp (or any app), then the chat.
  • Or tap Copy at the top and paste into a chat.

Put it on your Home Screen

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app.
  • It then opens full screen and works offline.
